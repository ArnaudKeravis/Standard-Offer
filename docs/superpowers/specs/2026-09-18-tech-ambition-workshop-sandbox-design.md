**Date:** 2026-09-18

**Source:** `TechAmbition_AIWorkshop_RunOfShow_v3_2026-09.pdf`

## Problem

The CoDesign & AI afternoon is a 16:9 room event (13:00-16:30) with timed rituals. A PDF cannot run Proud of, AI Clinics, or One Hour Back on the projector.

## Decisions

- One room projector. Facilitator drives the same 16:9 URL.
- Full afternoon replacement of the PPT.
- Phase runner plus rituals (2' Proud of, clinic rounds + table map, Hour Back steps).
- Hard-coded for this session. Not a workshop CMS.

## Shape

- Route: `/workshops/tech-ambition`
- Labs-style chassis (keyboard, swipe, HUD, fullscreen)
- CoDesign workshop look (Open Sans, navy/blue, phase colours)
- Client-only timers. Refresh resets. No backend.

## Out of scope

QR / live wall capture, speaker queue, saving owners, reusable workshop packs, Henri/Kevin slides beyond the v3 PDF.
