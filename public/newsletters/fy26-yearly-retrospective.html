Executive Summary

CoDesign & Labs is no longer a pilot. It is a commercial engine — embedded in major sales cycles, generating measurable uplift, and scaling globally.

In FY26 we moved from proving the concept to delivering against it. CoDesign actively progressed ~€1.1B of annualised contract value this year, across 8 confirmed wins and 13 strategic projects in delivery. The Sodexo Labs in Paris hosted 102 sessions — roughly half generated by client-facing CoDesign engagements (we bring clients in to anchor commitment), half by internal teams using the Labs to align on growth, sustainability and innovation. Workshops scored 4.37/5 average with an NPS of +53 across 43 surveyed participants, producing 1,000+ ideas, 50+ concepts and 20 client roadmaps. The model is now requested by 4 regions, with 2 US sites in framing.

Scaling the Labs globally

● FY27 Roadmap

This is not “prepare the Philadelphia session.” The ambition is higher: make our CoDesign method transferable and deployable in regions — by teams who are not designers (including IT). We bring 5 years of proven method, a CoDesign OS with 23 agents, Persona Studio, an Experience Catalogue, and credentials across a ~€2B perimeter. The year’s challenge: turn that into an acculturation and upskilling system others can own. Philadelphia (9 Sept · US Tech team) is our first pilot — not the endgame.
